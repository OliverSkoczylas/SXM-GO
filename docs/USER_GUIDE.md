# SXM GO — Sponsor User Guide

Welcome to SXM GO — the app that turns exploring St. Maarten into a game. Visitors earn points by checking in at beaches, restaurants, casinos, and attractions across the island. They compete on leaderboards, complete challenges, build daily streaks, and track their routes as they discover everything St. Maarten has to offer.

---

## Getting Started

### Creating an Account

1. Open the SXM GO app — you'll land on the Welcome screen.
2. Choose how you'd like to sign up:
   - **Email & Password** — enter your name, email, and a password.
   - **Continue with Google** — uses your existing Google account.
   - **Continue with Apple** — uses Face ID / Touch ID (iOS only).
   - **Continue with Facebook** — uses your Facebook account.
3. First-time users are guided through a 3-screen onboarding tutorial explaining check-ins, points, and challenges.

### Logging In

- Returning users are logged in automatically — the app remembers your session.
- Forgotten your password? Tap **Forgot Password** on the login screen and a reset link will be emailed to you.

## The Map

The Map is the home screen of SXM GO. It shows the entire island of St. Maarten with coloured pins for every featured location.

**Pin colours by category:**

| Colour | Category |
|--------|----------|
| 🔵 Blue | Beaches |
| 🟠 Orange | Restaurants |
| 🔴 Red | Casinos |
| 🟣 Purple | Attractions |
| 🩷 Pink | Shopping |
| 🟢 Green | Entertainment |

**What you can do on the Map:**

- Use the **category filter pills** at the top to show only one type of location (e.g. Beaches only).
- Tap **ℹ** on any location card (bottom list) to open the full Location Detail screen — description, opening hours, phone number, and website.
- Tap **Go** on any card to get turn-by-turn directions via Apple Maps or Google Maps.
- Tap **Check In** when you're physically at a location to earn points.
- Tap the green **🏃 button** (bottom-right corner) to start recording an activity route.
- Pull down on the location list to refresh.

---

## Checking In

Checking in is how you earn points and progress through challenges.

1. Walk to within **150 metres** of a location.
2. Tap the **Check In** button on the location card.
3. Points are awarded instantly and your profile updates in real time.

**Bonus points:**
- **First visit:** +20 bonus points, every time you visit a new location for the first time.
- **Daily streak:** Check in somewhere new each consecutive day — earn +5 pts/day up to a maximum of +50 pts bonus.
- **Itinerary completion:** Finish all stops in an itinerary to earn a **1.5× multiplier** on all points earned during it.

> **Anti-fraud note:** The system automatically detects GPS spoofing and mock locations. Suspicious check-ins are flagged for review but still provisionally awarded — so legitimate users are never blocked by false positives.

## Leaderboard

The Leaderboard lets you see how you stack up against all SXM GO users.

**Three leaderboard types:**
- **Global** — all-time total points, all users.
- **Weekly** — current week only, resets every Monday.
- **Monthly** — current month only.

Your own rank is highlighted so you can find it instantly.

**Group Leaderboards:**

Travel with friends or family? Create a private group:
1. Tap the group icon on the Leaderboard screen.
2. Choose **Create Group** (give it a name) or **Join with Invite Code**.
3. Share the 6-character invite code with your travel companions.
4. Switch to **Groups** view to see your private leaderboard.

## Itineraries

Plan your day with a custom itinerary — or follow a pre-made one.

**Creating an itinerary:**
1. Go to **Profile → My Itineraries → ＋ New Itinerary**.
2. Give it a title and optional description.
3. Add locations — the app automatically calculates the total distance and estimated completion time.
4. Set a difficulty level (Easy / Moderate / Challenging).

**Completing an itinerary:**
- As you check in at each stop, progress updates automatically.
- Finish all stops to trigger the **1.5× completion bonus**.

**Sharing:**
- Mark an itinerary as Public and share it with friends — they can duplicate it to their own profile.

## Activity Tracking

SXM GO can record your exploration route as you move around the island — similar to Strava.

1. Tap the green **🏃 button** on the Map screen.
2. The live tracker starts — your route is drawn as a red line on the map in real time.
3. Live stats display at the top: **Duration**, **Distance (km)**, **Pace (min/km)**.
4. When you're done, tap **Stop & Save**.
5. View all past activities in **Profile → Activity History**.
6. Tap any activity to see the full route map and stats.

## Friends & Social

**Adding friends:**
1. Go to **Profile → Friends**.
2. Tap the **Find People** tab and search by username.
3. Tap **Add Friend** — the other user receives a request.
4. Once accepted, you both appear in each other's Friends list.

**Managing friends:**
- **Friends tab:** See all your friends and their point totals.
- **Requests tab:** Accept or decline incoming requests; cancel sent requests.
- Tap any user's name anywhere in the app to view their public profile (points, badges, check-in count).

---

## Your Profile

Your profile is your personal dashboard.

- **Avatar** — tap to upload a photo from your camera roll.
- **Stats row** — total points and total check-ins at a glance.
- **Recent Activity** — your last 10 check-ins with location name, category, and points earned.
- **Achievements** — all badges you've earned, displayed as pills.
- **Menu:**
  - Friends
  - Activity History
  - My Itineraries
  - Settings
  - Privacy & Data
  - Change Password

To edit your display name or bio, tap **Edit Profile** on the profile screen.

## Push Notifications

SXM GO sends push notifications for:

- 🏅 Badge earned
- ✅ Challenge completed
- 📈 Leaderboard rank change
- 🤝 Friend request received

Customise which notifications you receive in **Profile → Settings → Notifications**.

---

## Offline Mode

No internet connection? SXM GO keeps working.

- Location data is cached for **24 hours** — you can still browse the full map and see all pins.
- Check-ins made while offline are **saved locally** and synced automatically the next time you connect.
- A banner at the top of the Map shows how many check-ins are pending sync.

---

## Privacy & Data

- Location is only collected with your **explicit permission**.
- You can pause location tracking at any time: **Profile → Privacy & Data → Disable Location**.
- To request full account deletion (GDPR/CCPA compliant): **Profile → Privacy & Data → Delete My Account**.
- All data is stored on secure cloud infrastructure with encrypted connections.
